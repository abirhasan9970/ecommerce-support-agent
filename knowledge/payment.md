# Payment Information

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- bKash
- Nagad
- Credit/Debit Card (Visa, Mastercard)
- Bank Transfer

## Cash on Delivery Limits
Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 5000 BDT. Orders above 5000 BDT must be prepaid.

## Payment Security
All online payments are processed through secure payment gateways. We never store your card details on our servers.

## Payment Confirmation
Once payment is successful, you will receive a confirmation via SMS and email (if email is provided).

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, no money will be deducted from your account. If money is deducted but the order shows as unpaid, please contact support with your transaction ID.

## Refunds
Please see the return and refund policy for information about refunds.
