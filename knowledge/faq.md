# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I place an order?
Browse products on our website, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You will receive an order confirmation once payment is successful.

## How can I contact customer support?
You can use this AI chat assistant anytime. For complex issues, request a human agent and a support ticket will be created for you.

## Do you offer discounts?
Yes. We run seasonal sales and occasional promotional offers. Discounts shown on the product page are the current available discounts. We do not offer additional discounts outside of listed promotions.

## Can I change my order after placing it?
You can request a change within 2 hours of placing the order, provided the order has not yet been shipped. Once shipped, the order cannot be modified.

## Do you ship outside Bangladesh?
No. Currently we only deliver within Bangladesh.

## What if I receive a damaged product?
Please contact support within 48 hours of delivery and provide a photo of the damaged item. We will arrange a replacement or refund according to our return policy.

## Is cash on delivery available?
Yes, cash on delivery is available for orders under a certain amount. Please see the payment information for details.
