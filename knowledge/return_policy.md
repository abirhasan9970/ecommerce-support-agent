# Return and Refund Policy

## Return Window
You can request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.

## Return Conditions
To be eligible for a return:
- The product must be unused and in its original packaging.
- The original receipt or order confirmation must be provided.
- The product must not be damaged due to customer misuse.

## Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned:
- Innerwear and personal hygiene products
- Customized or personalized items
- Products marked "Final Sale" on the product page

## Refund Method
- If paid by credit/debit card or mobile banking, refunds are processed to the original payment method.
- If paid by cash on delivery, refunds are processed via mobile banking.

## Refund Time
Refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

## Return Shipping Cost
- If the return is due to our mistake (wrong item, damaged item), we cover the return shipping cost.
- If the return is due to a customer preference change, the customer covers the return shipping cost.

## How to Start a Return
Contact support and provide your order ID. A support ticket will be created and our team will guide you through the process.
