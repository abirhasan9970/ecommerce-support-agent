# Delivery Information

## Delivery Charges
- Inside Dhaka city: 60 BDT
- Outside Dhaka (within Bangladesh): 120 BDT
- Orders above 2000 BDT: Free delivery inside Dhaka only

## Estimated Delivery Time
- Inside Dhaka: 1 to 2 business days
- Outside Dhaka: 3 to 5 business days
- Remote areas: 5 to 7 business days

## Delivery Partners
We use trusted local courier services. The exact courier is assigned at the time of shipment.

## Order Processing Time
Orders are usually processed within 24 hours on business days. Orders placed on Friday or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

## Delivery Restrictions
- We do not deliver to PO boxes.
- We only deliver within Bangladesh.
- If you are not available at the delivery address, the courier will attempt re-delivery once. After two failed attempts, the order may be returned to us.
