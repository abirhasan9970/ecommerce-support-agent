# Warranty Information

## Warranty Coverage
- Electronics: 6 months warranty from the date of delivery
- Home appliances: 12 months warranty from the date of delivery
- Clothing, footwear, and accessories: No warranty

## What Warranty Covers
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware failure under normal use
- Electrical faults (for eligible electronics)

## What Warranty Does Not Cover
- Physical damage due to drops or accidents
- Water damage
- Damage caused by unauthorized repair
- Normal wear and tear

## How to Claim Warranty
1. Contact support with your order ID and a description of the issue.
2. A support ticket will be created and reviewed by our warranty team.
3. If eligible, we will arrange repair, replacement, or refund according to the warranty terms.

## Warranty Transfer
Warranty is not transferable. It applies only to the original purchaser.
